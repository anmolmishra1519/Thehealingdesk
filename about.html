Psychology

Therapy is simply structured support.

Many people arrive at therapy unsure of what to expect. At its core, psychological support is a collaborative process — a space to slow down, understand your patterns, and build practical, realistic ways of coping with what life brings.

Not just for crises

Therapy isn't only for when things fall apart — it's equally valuable for growth, self-understanding and everyday stress.

A skill-building process

Conversations translate into practical tools — for regulating emotion, communicating clearly and navigating change.

Confidential, always

What's shared in a session stays there — a private, judgement-free space held entirely for you.

About Me

About Me

Ananyaa Mishra is a Counselling Psychologist working with individuals, psychology students and interns across Ayodhya, Noida, Delhi and online.

Her approach is warm and collaborative — rooted in genuinely listening before offering direction. Rather than following a fixed script, each session is shaped around the person in front of her: what they're carrying, what they need right now, and what would actually help.

Alongside individual therapy, Ananyaa works closely with psychology students and interns, helping translate classroom theory into grounded, real-world clinical understanding through case-based mentorship.

"Every session is shaped around the person in front of me — not a checklist."

— Ananyaa Mishra

Philosophy

My Approach

Therapy works best when it feels collaborative, honest and paced to the individual. The aim is always to create a space where you feel heard, understood and equipped — never judged.

Listen before I lead

Direction only comes after genuinely understanding what you're carrying — not before.

The person, not a label

Sessions are shaped around who you are and what you need — never reduced to a checklist or diagnosis.

Reflective and practical

Space to understand the "why", paired with concrete steps for the "what now".
