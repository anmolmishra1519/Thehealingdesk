About Thehealingdesk

A space to pause, reflect & understand yourself.

Thehealingdesk brings together psychological and psychiatric perspectives to support people navigating stress, transitions, relationships and the quieter, harder-to-name struggles of everyday life.

Care here is warm, collaborative and rooted in genuinely listening before offering direction. Support is available for adults seeking individual therapy, as well as psychology students and interns looking to build practical, real-world clinical understanding.

Psychology

Understanding the mind and behaviour through talk-based, evidence-informed therapy — helping you make sense of your thoughts, emotions and patterns.

Psychiatry

Understanding mental health from a medical perspective — clinical assessment and guidance for concerns that may need evaluation alongside therapy.

Every session is shaped around the person, not a checklist. This is a space where you're welcome exactly as you are.
