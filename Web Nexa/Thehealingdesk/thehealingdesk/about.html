Psychology

Therapy is simply structured support.

Many people arrive at therapy unsure of what to expect. At its core, psychological support is a collaborative process — a space to slow down, understand your patterns, and build practical, realistic ways of coping with what life brings.

Not just for crises

Therapy isn't only for when things fall apart — it's equally valuable for growth, self-understanding and everyday stress.

A skill-building process

Conversations translate into practical tools — for regulating emotion, communicating clearly and navigating change.

Confidential, always

What's shared in a session stays there — a private, judgement-free space held entirely for you.

Consultant Psychiatrist / Neuropsychiatrist

Dr. Sunny Ambavat

Consultant Psychiatrist / Neuropsychiatrist

Dr. Sunny Ambavat is a Consultant Psychiatrist providing individualised psychiatric care for adults, older adults, adolescents and families in Gurugram and through online consultations.

His approach is centred on understanding the person behind the symptoms, identifying the factors contributing to their difficulties, and developing a treatment plan collaboratively.

His areas of particular clinical interest include geriatric mental health, sexual health and substance-use disorders. Every consultation is individualised, collaborative and centred around your overall wellbeing.

Who He Works With

Mental health concerns can affect people at different stages of life. Dr. Sunny works with individuals and families experiencing:

  • Depression and other mood disorders
  • Anxiety and related difficulties
  • Bipolar disorder
  • Psychotic disorders
  • Dementia and cognitive difficulties
  • Substance-use disorders
  • Neurodevelopmental conditions
  • Behavioural and emotional difficulties

He also has an interest in working with older adults, children and adolescents, people with chronic medical illnesses, and individuals experiencing mental-health difficulties during the perinatal period.

Services

  • Depression — Persistent low mood, loss of interest, tiredness, changes in sleep or appetite, difficulty concentrating, or feelings of hopelessness.
  • Anxiety & Panic — Persistent worry, restlessness, muscle tension, palpitations, panic attacks or feeling unable to switch off your thoughts.
  • Bipolar Disorder — Episodes of depression alternating with periods of unusually elevated mood, increased energy, reduced need for sleep, racing thoughts or impulsive behaviour.
  • Psychotic Disorders — Hearing or seeing things others do not, strongly held unusual beliefs, disorganized speech, social withdrawal, or reduced motivation.
  • Substance-Use Disorders — Craving, difficulty reducing or stopping substance use, increasing tolerance, withdrawal symptoms, or repeated relapse.
  • ADHD & Neurodevelopmental Concerns — Problems with sustained attention, organisation, impulsivity, excessive activity, forgetfulness, or academic difficulties.
  • Memory & Behavioural Changes — In older adults: increasing forgetfulness, confusion, impaired self-care, changes in personality or behaviour, agitation or mood symptoms.
  • Sexual & Relationship Health — Confidential assessment for erectile difficulties, premature ejaculation, low sexual desire, painful sexual activity, or medication-related sexual difficulties.

Treatment Approach

Psychiatric treatment is individualised according to the person's concerns, clinical needs and preferences. Depending on the situation, treatment may include:

  • Psychiatric medication, when appropriate
  • Brief psychological interventions
  • Motivational enhancement for substance-use concerns
  • Psychoeducation and behavioural strategies
  • Sexual-health interventions
  • Support for older adults and their families
  • Coordination with psychological and other healthcare services

What Happens During a Consultation

  1. 01 — Understanding your concerns — We begin by understanding what has been happening, how long you have been experiencing it, and how it is affecting your everyday life.
  2. 02 — Clinical assessment — Relevant psychological, medical, medication, sleep, substance-use and social factors are explored as appropriate.
  3. 03 — Making sense of the difficulties — The assessment helps develop an understanding of the difficulties and, where appropriate, a clinical diagnosis.
  4. 04 — Planning treatment together — Available treatment options are discussed so that the approach can be tailored to your needs.
  5. 05 — Follow-up — Treatment response, progress, concerns and any necessary changes are reviewed over time.
